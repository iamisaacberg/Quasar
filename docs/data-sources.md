## Local acquisition checkpoint

Run `python3 scripts/acquire_local_data.py` to refresh the Census county geometry, USGS stream-location metadata and latest county-intersecting USGS 3DEP 10 m tile. The command also downloads the current FEMA NFHL county package when it is absent or invalid. Files, retrieval timestamps, source identifiers, checksums, CRS, units and processing notes are saved under ignored `data/raw/` and `data/processed/source_manifest.json`. It does not access BigQuery, fetch parcel/owner/address data, or connect a live alert feed. The NFHL and DEM rasters are research inputs only; they are not yet clipped/normalized into the scoring pipeline.

## Isaias 2026 replay sources

- [NHC Isaias 2026 archive](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/ISAIAS.shtml): `scripts/prepare_isaias.py` acquires original public advisories with source checksums and issue timestamps. NOAA context does not establish local flood observations.
- [Miami-Dade street centerlines, layer 8](https://gisweb.miamidade.gov/arcgis/rest/services/LandManagement/MD_ZoningLandManagementViewer/MapServer/8): minimal road identifiers/names and geometry only. A curated subset connects fictional sites and destinations, with full county-segment validation. Local research artifacts remain ignored; review county terms before redistribution. Topology, speeds, access connectors and restrictions are exercise assumptions.
- [WeatherNext BigQuery](https://developers.google.com/weathernext/guides/bigquery): the separate historical importer reads October 2026 initialization partitions using dry-run defaults and a byte cap. Live historical acquisition verified October 17: five six-hourly initializations for the October 9 Miami-time replay, 540 hourly site rows. The completed Sandbox query used 383,408,668,672 bytes under a reviewed 384 GB cap; billing was disabled. All displayed six-hour rainfall windows are complete; future acquisition gaps remain unknown. Source hourly means, native 0.1° geometry, units and import time survive reconstruction. Historical data terms specify CC BY 4.0.

See [Isaias setup](isaias-demo.md) for commands, provenance and limitations. Acquisition state is reported from local files at runtime.
